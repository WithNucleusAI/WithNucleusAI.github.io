Why Tween

One consistent API for scroll, SVG, text and UI — built on a core that never gets in your way.

No dependencies, no jank, no wrestling with the browser. Sequence anything on a timeline and ship motion that feels engineered, not decorated.

Pick your discipline

Scroll

Scroll-driven storytelling, without the jank.

Pin sections, scrub timelines, snap to scenes, and parallax anything. The scroll position becomes your playhead — everything else stays buttery.

SVG

Draw, morph, and move vectors like clay.

Animate strokes as they draw themselves, morph one shape into another, and send elements along any path — resolution-independent and pixel-perfect.

Explore SVG
Text

Split, scramble, and reveal type.

Break headlines into characters, words, or lines and choreograph each one. Typewriters, scrambles, staggered reveals — typography that performs.

Explore Text
UI

Flip between states with silky transitions.

Reorder grids, expand cards, swap layouts — record a state, make your change, and let the library animate the difference. Layout thrash, solved.
