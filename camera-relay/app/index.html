Your camera, joining from anywhere

with your camera in California.

Some meeting sites only let people in from a specific country. This console walks you — step by step — through joining on a Mac that's in that country, while the face and voice on screen come from your own computer somewhere else. No jargon, just fill in a few boxes and follow the checklist.

The meeting opens on the India Mac, so the site sees an Indian connection. Your camera is streamed in and shows up there as if it were that Mac's own webcam. You sit in California and control the Mac from your desk.

Try the easy fix first. If the country block looks like a glitch, ask whoever runs the meeting to turn it off — that's usually one setting on their end and skips all of this. And make sure joining this way is okay for your meeting before you set it up.

What you'll need

About 20 minutes to set up once. After that, starting a call takes two minutes.

  • A Mac in the country you need to appear from — a friend's or home machine on a normal home internet connection.
  • A small rented server (about $5/month) that passes the video along. A tech-savvy friend can set this up once, or follow Step 2.
  • A remote-control app (like Chrome Remote Desktop or AnyDesk) so you can see and use that Mac from California.
  • Two free apps on the Mac — OBS and BlackHole — which the console links you to.

Join the meeting

            
The meeting is on a private site, so you'll join in a browser on the Mac.

            
    
              
  1. Use Chrome, not Safari
    Safari hides the virtual camera. Chrome (or Firefox) shows it.
    2. 
              
  2. Make sure OBS Virtual Camera is already running
    Start it in OBS before opening the meeting site. If the site is already open, reload the page.
    3. 
              
  3. Open the meeting link and click "Allow"
    When Chrome asks for camera and microphone, allow it.
    4. 
              
  4. Choose your devices
    Camera → OBS Virtual Camera. Microphone → BlackHole 2ch. Speaker → the Mac's normal output, so your remote-control app plays the meeting back to you. If the site has no camera menu, click the little camera icon in Chrome's address bar to pick it.
    5. 
            

          


          

            

              
              
You're set. Your face and voice come from California; the meeting sees the India Mac. Keep OBS running for the whole call.
